## Overview

Folds the three DeepGEMM MegaMoE expert implementations into one `DeepGemmMegaMoEExperts` layer. The three are DeepSeek V4/V4.1's `DeepseekV4MegaMoEExperts`, Kimi K3's `KimiK3MegaMoEExperts`, and the `DeepGemmMegaMoEBackend` pair under both. The new layer is driven by a feature matrix: routed precision × activation × fusable shared-expert precision. The PR also adds NVFP4 routed experts (DeepGEMM `nvfp4_mega_moe`) as a new row. It keeps the procedure from #60096: weights load straight into the DeepGEMM layout, with no post-load transform.

**Draft:** the end-to-end eval has not been run yet (see Validation).

## Claims

- **One layer.** Every DeepGEMM MegaMoE caller uses `forward(hidden_states, topk_weights, topk_ids, shared_experts=None)`.
- **Fusion decided at construction.** Fusion of the shared expert is decided once, from the shared MLP's weights, and never changes at runtime.
- **Supported matrix.** It covers every routed precision and activation that the pinned DeepGEMM exposes:

  | routed | kernel | dispatch | fusable shared | activations |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | mxfp4 | `fp8_fp4_mega_moe` (fp8xfp4) | MXFP8 | MXFP8 | swiglu,
