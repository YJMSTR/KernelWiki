## Why

The Triton unified-attention kernel casts softmax probabilities to `V.dtype` before `P @ V`. With an FP8 KV cache, small probabilities can underflow to zero and lose meaningful contributions to the attention output.

## Summary

- Scale `P` before its FP8 cast and undo the scale after the FP32 dot. The scale is 256 for NVIDIA E4M3 and 128 for other FP8 formats.
- Add a focused regression test for an `exp(-8)` probability that previously became zero. Existing tests and tolerances are unchanged.

This is the same [low-precision scaling technique](https://github.com/vllm-project/flash-attention/blob/2e3a6694ba2f0d8e106f28d2a5d3f6050ebf1572/flash_attn/cute/flash_fwd_sm100.py#L85-L90) used by FlashAttention's Blackwell kernel. Its 8-bit path sets [`max_offset = 8`](https://github.com/vllm-project/flash-attention/blob/2e3a6694ba2f0d8e106f28d2a5d3f6050ebf1572/flash_attn/cute/flash_fwd_sm100.py#L2189-L2209), equivalent to scaling `P` by 256 before the low-precision dot.
