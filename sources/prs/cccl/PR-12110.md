## Description

Context:

- https://github.com/NVIDIA/cccl/issues/11921

During the application of clang-tidy `misc-header-cleaner`, I realized that we can simplify/consolidate the CUB dependencies related to the `triple_chevron_launch`.

There are 20 headers in CUB that include `thrust/system/cuda/detail/core/triple_chevron_launch.h`. The PR removes all of them and consolidates kernel launch in `cub/detail/launcher/cuda_runtime.cuh` by relying on `cuda::launch` or `TripleChevronFactory`.
This also adds the check related to PDL (`__assert_pdl_allowed`).

The modifications in `cub/cub/config.cuh` and `cub/cub/cub.cuh` are comments only
<!-- upstream-excerpt-end -->
