## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
Replaces the default policy of `cub::DeviceReduce::ReduceByKey` (used when no specific tuning exists) with the one of the former Thrust implementation. I traced back the origin of those tunings, and CUB's tuning comes from sm_35, whereas Thrust's comes from sm_52.

This is needed to avoid benchmark regressions when porting `thrust::reduce_by_key` to CUB (#5583, #3942).
