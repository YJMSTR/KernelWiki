## Description

Closes https://github.com/NVIDIA/cccl/issues/11971

Supersedes #11980.

This PR restores DeviceBatchedTopK to its pre-#11835 performance for most workloads and shrinks the slowdown at the most affected sizes, segments of 512K and 1M keys, from about 14% to about 2% on average on B200, while keeping the `-0.0 == +0.0` ranking that #11835 introduced.

Follow-up to #11835, which made `DeviceBatchedTopK` rank `-0.0` and `+0.0` as equal under `prefer_smaller_index` and `prefer_larger_index`. That fix in #11835 appends `ProcessFloatMinusZero` to the key twiddle in the two radix functors (`extract_bin_op_t` and `identify_candidates_op_t`), so it runs on every key in every radix pass and again in the final filter. On B200 that costs about 6% on average over the segmented top-k benchmark and up to 14% for segments of 1M keys.

This PR recoups that with two small changes:

1. **A twiddle that normalizes for free.** `Traits<T>::TwiddleIn` complements _negative_ floats, which leaves `-0.0` at `0x7FFF...`, one below `+0.0` at `0x8000...`.
