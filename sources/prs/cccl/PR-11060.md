## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
This PR makes a couple of performance improvements for our AIR-based BlockTopK specialization.

## A few items I am planning to leave as follow-ups:

- Blocked versus Striped arrangement: This is higher on my priority list. I think striped could unlock performance improvements. I'm thinking of exposing striped through a `striped_to_strped`-suffix overload, e.g., `max_pairs_striped_to_strped`. I think we have a precedent in block sort and exchange.
- Wiring up template parameters of different BlockTopK-specializations: I am not clear yet on how we will want to expose parameter knobs of different specializations through a common `BlockTopK` interface. I would wait for that until we have most of the specializations in, then start thinking about this as part of exposing `BlockTopK` publicly.
