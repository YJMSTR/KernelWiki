## Description

I was inspired by a recent internal presentation on sorting. I had a brainwave and remembered that @Nyrio did some amazing work introducing Pairwise Sorting Networks to CUB https://github.com/NVIDIA/cccl/pull/1552. The original PR was too broad, and the lack of infrastructure on our side (i.e. device-side benchmarking) held up his work.

The PR breaks down and modernizes the original work by introducing the Pairwise Thread-sort only (can be extended to warp, block, etc.). The results for key-value sorting are amazing!
Besides segmented sort, the algorithm could be also relevant to Top-K @elstehle

Here the speedups compared to `OddEvenSort`

<img width="1664" height="635" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-18 121920" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6380720e-263e-48cb-84cf-703603bd83b5" />

Another nice feature is that Pairwise sort allows to operate on larger sets, namely after 54 items the performance of OddEvenSort collapsed (reg spilling) and the speedup is three order of magnitude larger.
