## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
This adds a convenience helper for doing any/all reductions. Lots of existing code does `tl.sum(pred) != 0` for any, but it turns out that the min/max redux operations are more efficient than the add operation so this results in higher latency:

Elements | Min/max, ns/iteration | Sum, ns/iteration | Speedup
-- | -- | -- | --
32 | 22.6 | 36.6 | 1.62×
128 | 57.4 | 81.8 | 1.43×
512 | 71.0 | 102.5 | 1.44×
2048 | 110.2 | 140.7 | 1.28×
8192 | 265.8 | 283.7 | 1.07×

Building the faster method into the stdlib should encourage its use more broadly.
<!-- upstream-excerpt-end -->
