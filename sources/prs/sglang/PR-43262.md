## Motivation

DeepSeek-V4.1 MegaMoE decode and verify spend several launches on the gate GEMM, the sqrtsoftplus top-k and the routing copy; DeepGEMM's `bf16_mega_gate` does this in one kernel.

## Modifications

- `SGLANG_OPT_DEEPGEMM_MEGA_GATE` routes MegaMoE decode/verify (up to 2048 rows) through `bf16_mega_gate`, including vision-biased and hash-routed layers and the padded-row mask.
- Unless routed experts are being captured, the gate writes straight into MegaMoE's routing buffer and the pre-dispatch only quantizes (`kCopyTopk`). EPLB static and dynamic placements are applied in-kernel via `to_physical_map`/`logical_count` (dynamic spreads replicas by rank and token instead of `randint`), and the expert distribution recorder reads the buffer. `lp`/`fake` dispatch falls back to the regular router.
- `SGLANG_SIMULATE_*` keeps the gate on: the balanced override runs after it, masking padded rows and applying the routed scale in its one launch.
- Tests: `test_mega_gate.py` (kernel vs reference under CUDA graph replay), a masked/scaled case in `test_round_robin_ep_balance.py`, and MegaGate unit tests in `test_mega_moe_deepgemm_api.py`.
