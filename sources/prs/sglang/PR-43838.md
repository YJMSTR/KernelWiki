## Motivation

DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash small-batch decode fusions (fused all-reduce + mHC epilogues, the fused inverse-RoPE + WO-A + MXFP8 kernel, and SM100 swap-AB decode attention) were gated to TP4 shapes (tp_size == 4, 2 local o_groups, 16 local heads). TP2 decode therefore ran the unfused path at every batch size, although TP2 is the better latency/throughput point for this model on GB300 at mid concurrency.

## Modifications

- Fused all-reduce + mHC: admit tp_size 2 (the all-reduce kernels are already templated on world size).
- Fused WO-A: take the local group count (2 at TP4, 4 at TP2) as a template parameter.
- Swap-AB decode attention: run TP2's 32 local heads as two 16-head groups on the partial kernel's grid z axis.
- Tests: WO-A fused test parametrized over 2 and 4 groups; swap-AB test covers 32 heads.

The gates check shapes (16 or 32 local heads, 2 or 4 local o_groups, tp_size 2 or 4), not TP size alone, so any deployment with those local shapes takes these paths; only DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash TP2 and TP4 have been validated.
