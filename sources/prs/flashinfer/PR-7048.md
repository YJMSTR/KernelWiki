## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
`deepgemm_dense_mqa` (DeepGEMM dense MQA logits, the FP8 MQA logits used by the DeepSeek-V3.2 indexer) has four Cake variants. They are dense FP8 logits with 32 heads, dense FP8 logits with 64 heads (grid-stride tiers, one-split, tile-schedule and in-kernel KV-split forms), dense FP4 (e2m1, block-scaled) logits with 32 heads, and paged FP8 MQA logits. This PR delivers the two dense FP8 variants. The 32-head programs are re-exported with new per-shape text options (7 programs added, 4 removed). The 64-head in-kernel KV-split path now also serves whole-grid chunk counts for K > 2048, which retires the two `le1024` tier routes. The baseline is the previous Cake export of the same kernel on FlashInfer main 0179e5b312d6. On GB300 the geomean speedup over 86 shapes is 1.0053 (min 0.9930, max 1.0785): 1.0085 over the 32 dense FP8 32-head shapes and 1.0035 over the 54 causal-prefill shapes (52 at 64 heads, 2 at 32 heads). On B200 the geomean speedup over 86 shapes is 1.0043 (min 0.9751, max 1.0577): 1.0082 over the 32 dense FP8 32-head shapes and 1.0020 over the 54 causal-prefill shapes.
