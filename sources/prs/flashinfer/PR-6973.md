## Summary

This PR is the round-2 deslop of the Cake "mega" family for SM100 / SM103, tracked in #6669. It covers three experimental packages: the fused MoE routing gate, the mHC fused norm / mix kernels and MegaMoE v3. Commits 1, 2 and 5 keep one host binding per launch shape. One mHC binding serves all 14 mHC programs, 9 of the 16 routing gate launchers serve all gate programs, and two MegaMoE pipeline launchers replace four others. Commit 3 points the ten kept gate and mHC bindings at the shared Cake host header and deletes the two per-family copies. Commit 4 folds the four 40-split mHC kernel sources into their 27-split twins. Commit 6 keeps each repeated launch argument plan once. Commit 7 deletes dead preparation branches and an unread helper. Every former kernel symbol stays defined for its program.
