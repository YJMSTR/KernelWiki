## 📌 Description

VC-Attention-QK16 kernels and optimizations for the PrimTS dense context FMHA. This will be later productionized for TRT-LLM VisualGen.
The implementation follows the [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.15810) but uses QK-BF16 BMM1 and PV-FP8 rather than QK-INT8/PV-FP8.

- **VC-Attention-QK16 kernel.** bf16 Q/K on the 16-bit MMA; V as E4M3 residuals around per-tile
  means; P through the ExpCast E4M3 path. Plan/run follow the Sage attention interface
  (`vc_config`, `vc=`). K/V preparation is two CuTe DSL kernels behind `VCAttentionPreprocessor`.
  Dense bf16 and fp8 paths unchanged.
- **Two V treatments, chosen at plan time.**
  - *Tile means* (`VCAttentionConfig()`): the kernel restores each tile's mean inside the online
    softmax with batched UMMA steps on O. V-Smooth demeaning follows the paper's step schedule.
  - *Repair tiles* (`VCAttentionConfig(repair_tiles=N)`): No means. The highest-residual tokens
