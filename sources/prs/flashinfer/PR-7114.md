<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
`flashinfer.mega_mhc` (`prepare_mega_mhc`, programs under `flashinfer/experimental/deepgemm_mega_mhc`) contains programs for the fused manifold-constrained hyper-connection (mHC) inference step. Before this PR, it ships 15 programs:

- **Existing variants:** 14 hidden-5120 FP8-E4M3-output variants (shifted / unshifted, shared-scale, `col` / `extra` scale layouts, split counts 16 / 20 / 27 / 40) on `sm_100a` / `sm_103a`, and one hidden-4096 BF16-output variant (`sf_layout="bf16"`) on `sm_107a`.
- **This PR:** two hidden-4096 quantized-output variants on `sm_107a`:
  - `sf_layout="fp8_group128"` also stores `y_fp8` (FP8-E4M3, `[T, 4096]`) and `y_fp8_sf` (FP32, `[T, 32]`, one scale per 128-column group per token).
