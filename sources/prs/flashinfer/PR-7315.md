## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
FlashInfer's Cake fused-MoE kernels come in four forms: two SiTU routed-MoE forms (NVFP4 weights; MXFP4 weights with MXFP8 activations, in CUDA C++ and CuTe DSL lowerings), the warp-decode MoE, and, with this PR, the BF16 activation × NVFP4 weight "W4A16 Split" form. It runs FC1 with gated SwiGLU and FC2 as two kernels behind `cutlass_fused_moe(backend="cake")` on the prequantized W4A16 weight tensors, with 10 programs per architecture. No earlier Cake W4A16 version exists, so the baseline is FlashInfer's own CuTe DSL W4A16 split fused-MoE at its tuned tactic. Against that baseline, 6 of the 12 shapes are faster in both sessions, up to 1.054 / 1.046 at D1. The other 6 are slower: D8, D32, D128, D512, D4096 and G128. The largest gap is at G128 (0.923).

NVIDIA B300 (SXM6, sm_103a), CUDA 13.4. Preset D = H 7168, I 2048, E 256, top-8 on 1 GPU; preset G = H 6144, I 2048, E 256, top-8, EP8 on 8 GPUs. Both arms CUDA-graph captured, CUPTI kernel time, cold L2, mean of 4 identical instances, two sessions s1/s2. Ratio = FlashInfer µs / Cake µs (>1 = Cake faster).

| preset | T | FlashInfer µs s1 / s2 | Cake µs s1 / s2 | FI/Cake s1 / s2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D (H7168, 1 GPU) | 1 | 53.73
