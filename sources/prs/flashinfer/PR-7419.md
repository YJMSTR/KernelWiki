## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
FlashInfer's `mm_bf16_fp4` multiplies BF16 activations by FP4 (E2M1) weights that carry E4M3 block scales, with FP32 accumulation. Cake ships it as two backend variants that differ in operand layout: `blackwell-native` (cuDNN-ABI layouts, BF16 or FP16 output) and `blackwell-tiled` (CuTe-ABI prepared-tile layouts, BF16 output). This PR regenerates 10 of the kernel's 97 Cake programs, with bit-identical outputs. The three two-CTA M=128 tcgen05 programs for 768/2112/2048 (`blackwell-native` with and without alpha, `blackwell-tiled` with alpha) now free the pair's tensor memory while the last stores are in flight, instead of after a final cluster-wide barrier. The other seven are `blackwell-tiled` register-streaming small-M programs. The split-K ones (4-CTA mailbox, 8-CTA reduce-scatter, 6-way, each with and without alpha) now load and convert all their block scales once before the main loop, and the single-CTA and mailbox programs with alpha read alpha right after the dependent-launch wait. Speedup is the previous Cake generation's time on `main` (#7376) divided by this PR's time, both from the same GPU in one job. On GB300 / B200 it is 1.099x / 1.085x for `blackwell-native` and 1.074
