## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
This PR adds SM120 support to the Cake backend for QSA NVFP4 decode, `flashinfer.experimental.cake_qsa_nvfp4`. Callers reach it through `flashinfer.sparse.qsa_sparse_paged_attention(..., backend="cake")`, and the default `backend="auto"` picks it on the supported compute capabilities. The kernel runs gated sparse paged GQA decode attention of BF16 queries over an NVFP4 paged KV cache. The shape is Hq 24 / Hkv 2 / D 256, with 2051 selected tokens per row in 64-token tiles and page 16. It supports two KV layouts (NHD and HND) and two call forms: split (decode + merge) and fused (cooperative decode alone). Six programs serve these forms:

- the mma.sync K-first decode
- its whole-tile build
- the mma.sync cooperative decode, which merges its splits in the launch (the fused form)
- the tcgen05 decode
- the tcgen05 cooperative decode
- the split-KV merge

This PR delivers the SM120 (compute capability 12.0) libraries of the plain and cooperative mma.sync decodes and of the merge. The SM120 part has no tcgen05 instructions and no tensor memory, so the mma.sync programs serve it. Compute capabilities 8.x, 9.0, 10.0, 10.3 and 10.7 were delivered before; 12.1 is not covered.
