## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
This Cake attention kernel comes in three variants. All three do dense non-causal MHA with D = 128, take BF16 in and give BF16 out, use FP32 softmax and accept any strides (NHD or HND views). Each variant is one call that runs a chain of statistics, quantization and attention kernels, and it smooths and quantizes the inputs on the GPU on every call. The variants are NVFP4 QK + NVFP4 PV on SM100 / SM103, INT8 QK + NVFP4 PV on SM120 and INT8 QK + FP8 E4M3 PV on SM90. This PR delivers the SM90 variant as `flashinfer/experimental/cake_fp8_attention` (sm_90a), through `flashinfer.prefill.prepare_fp8_attention(q, k, v, out, backend="cake")` and `fp8_attention_workspace_bytes`. It does INT8 QK with K-mean smoothing and FP8 E4M3 PV in a chain of four kernels: statistics partials, statistics finalize, quantization and attention. We measured the complete BF16-in / BF16-out call (statistics + quantization + attention under CUDA-graph replay) against the baseline's complete call. Against FlashAttention-3 BF16 on H100, it is 1.08x–1.16x faster for R2–R7 and 0.86x on R1.
