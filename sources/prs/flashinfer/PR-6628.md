## Kimi-K3 NVFP4 SiTU experts: SM100 8- and 16-token rows with evict-first weight-stream loads

On SM100 (B200), the 8-token row now runs `n8_feature_ef_f2e` and the 16-token row now runs `n8_w2a_m16_ef_f2e`. Each new route keeps the four launches of the previous route. Only the FC1 and FC2 programs change. The new programs put an `evict_first` cache hint on their weight-stream TMA loads, which are A (the weights) and SFA (the weight scale factors). A row reads the weights and their scale factors once, so they should not displace the activations and routing tables that are read again. SM103 (B300) is unchanged. The single-token rows of both architectures are also unchanged.

| 8 | `n8_feature`: `quant_route:s2b`, `fc1:n8_s3b`, `fc2:n8_workfeed`, `finalize:feature` | **`n8_feature_ef_f2e`**: `quant_route:s2b`, `fc1:n8_persistent_ef`, `fc2:n8_workfeed_f2e`, `finalize:feature` | `n8_feature` (unchanged) |
