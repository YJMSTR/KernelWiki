## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
> Developed by the **CAKE team**, the Top-K select programs in this PR **outperform** the programs they replace on 3 of the 6 distinct
> frozen Top-K shapes per architecture (1.18–2.28×: the `t1024` shape now served by the new 32-query instance, the tied `t256` shape now
> served by the new all-equal-fold instance, and the `k16384` shape on the regenerated 16-query program under the refit plan) and read PARITY
> on the other 3 (`t64` / `t8` on the unchanged `topk_select_streaming` program; the ~5 µs stage-only `t3` shape, which runs the regenerated
> 16-query program on an unchanged plan, 0.9999 / 0.9937). Over the 10 frozen manifest rows (each `hkt` shape has a `thk` twin row that
> re-measures the same kernel call): **WIN 5 / PARITY 5 / LOSS 0** per architecture, with **bit-identical selected indices** on every row.
> The A/B ratios were timed through the Cake generator's dispatcher on integration head `cc6f5d27`, whose Top-K program text is
> byte-identical to this PR's files; the shipped FlashInfer modules were checked against that dispatcher by the export protocol under
> Validation. Stacked on #6397 (decode family); the decode files are unchanged by this PR.
