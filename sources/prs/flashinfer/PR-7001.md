## 📌 Description

Add opt-in NVFP4/NVFP4 and MXFP8/MXFP8 backends from a combined Blackwell export. Standard inference and GenPhase share the FC12 epilogue and extension, with separate mainloops. Existing backend defaults and legacy BF16 paths stay in place.

The standard adapter forwards parameterized SwiGLU, bounded or unbounded SiTU, early or late routing weights, and static, persistent or deferred finalization. NVFP4 also supports per-token FC1 activation scales and its existing CAKE finalizer. Invalid routes are masked in BF16 and quantized combine.

The export also fixes packed routing bounds, missing NVFP4 scale checks, FC1 scale padding in AOT, and overlapping grid-stride scheduler work.

Select `sm100_nvfp4_nvfp4_bf16_cutedsl_next` or `sm100_mxfp8_mxfp8_bf16_cutedsl_next`. Source and exporter revisions are recorded in `VENDOR.md`.
