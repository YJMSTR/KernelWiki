## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
`cake_kimi_k3_mla` has one Cake variant: FP8 E4M3 query and paged FP8 E4M3 latent cache (512 latent + 64 rope = 576, page 64), with BF16 output. The variant uses the following programs. Each one is a single source shared by sm_100a and sm_103a:

- Row-tile decode for 16, 32, 48, 64 and 96 packed rows.
- A two-CTA wide decode.
- Split-KV reducers: a warp reducer (1, 2 or 4 warps per row) and a CTA reducer.

This PR delivers:

- The 16-, 32- and 48-row tiles, with a late launch trigger for the reducer. The late trigger is used for split plans with at least 32 main CTAs.
- Warp and CTA reducers that compute their row's setup before they wait for the attention grid.
- A one-wave CTA reducer route with full partial prefetch.

The 64- and 96-row tiles and the wide program are unchanged.

The baseline ("previous") is the previous Cake version of this kernel, which FlashInfer main ships. "new" is this PR. On both GPUs most shapes are ties within measurement noise, and no shape is slower:

- **GB300:** faster on 7 of 41 shapes (shapes 1, 2, 3, 12, 13, 33 and 36) and a tie on the other 34.
- **B200:** faster on 3 of 41 shapes (shapes 1, 2 and 30) and a tie on the other 38.
