## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
The kernel behind `MiniMaxH3Nvfp4PreAttention` (`flashinfer.cake_minimax_h3`) is the MiniMax-H3 fused pre-attention with NVFP4 input. It has two stages: a norm stage (RMSNorm, AdaLN, NVFP4 quantize) and an NVFP4 QKV GEMM with a fused epilogue (Q/K RMSNorm, NeoX RoPE, destination-major pack). It has 4 variants: 2 output formats (`out_format="nvfp4"`, the default, and `out_format="e4m3"`) on 2 architectures (sm_100a and sm_103a). This PR delivers all 4 variants on sm_100a and sm_103a, with a faster norm stage and a lower-overhead launch. The baseline is the previous Cake version of the kernel, which is the current FlashInfer route of this entry point. On GB300 the speedup is 1.0257x to 1.1198x with nvfp4 output and 1.0274x to 1.1140x with e4m3 output. On GB200 it is 1.0010x to 1.0526x with nvfp4 output and 1.0139x to 1.0603x with e4m3 output.

**NVIDIA GB300, sm_103a, CUDA 13.4 toolkit, PyTorch cu130, power limit 1400 W.** The tables show GPU kernel time per operator call, which is the busy time of the call's kernels with launch gaps excluded.
