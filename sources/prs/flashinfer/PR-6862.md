## Summary

This PR covers the four Cake-generated FP8 families from #6660. All four are JIT-built, and `flashinfer/aot.py` also builds the grouped and SiLU-quant SM100 modules:
- the batch GEMM in `csrc/cake_batch_deepgemm_fp8/`, behind `batch_deepgemm_fp8_nt_groupwise(backend="cake")` (38 programs, 67 modules across sm_100a and sm_103a)
- the grouped GEMM in `csrc/cake_grouped_fp8_gemm/`, behind `prepare_group_gemm_fp8_nt_groupwise_contiguous` (15 programs)
- the grouped GEMM with fused SiLU quantisation in `csrc/cake_grouped_fp8_fused_silu_quant/`, behind `prepare_group_gemm_fp8_nt_groupwise_contiguous_silu_quant` (5 programs)
- the opt-in grouped MXFP8 quantize in `csrc/cake_grouped_mxfp8_quantize/`, behind `mxfp8_grouped_quantize(backend="cake")` (2 programs)

The PR has five commits. They drop dead host text, build the fp16 mxfp8 program from the bf16 source, replace the batch loader's two rendered route chains with a 56-row data table, move the repeated device preamble
