## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
> Developed by the **CAKE team**, the kernels in this PR **outperform** the relevant **SOTA baselines at the time of merge** on the **vast majority of performance benchmark shapes**, matching them otherwise.

This PR cleans up the Cake FMHA backend in `csrc/cake_fmha` for #6636 with fourteen commits, in the order listed under "Commits". Commit 1 fixes the BF16 paged decode backend so that it writes a caller-supplied LSE at the b4 and b128 exact shapes. Before this fix, it left that buffer unwritten. Commits 2 to 9 fold each generated clone family into one source per family, selected by the `-D` defines that the loaders pass; commits 3, 8 and 9 add the defines that were missing. The families are: the generic `decode_native_bf16`, `decode_native_fp16_nhd` and `decode_native_fp16_hd512` members; the `context_fp8` and `context_bf16` members, followed by a merge of the two `context_bf16` per-arch files; the `decode_quant` page-size pairs; the small-M hd256 decode components; the balanced decode components; and the DCP static programs together with the DCP balanced fold0/fold1 programs. Commit 10 is host-only: it removes three undefined DCP launch declarations and unread DCP registry met
