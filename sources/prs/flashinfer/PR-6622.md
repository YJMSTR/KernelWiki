### Kimi-K3 NVFP4 SiTU experts: SM100 8192-token row with fused quantization + reset and 512-pair routing CTAs

On SM100 (B200), the 8192-token row of the Kimi-K3 NVFP4 SiTU experts now runs `large_q8i_p512fh2qr` instead of `large_q8i`, which #6341 shipped for the SM100 large rows. The new route launches seven kernels instead of eight. One kernel now does both the activation quantization and the routing-table reset. The routing histogram and scatter are replaced by variants that handle 512 routed (token, expert-slot) pairs per CTA. FC1, FC2 and the finalize are unchanged. SM103 (B300) is unchanged, and the SM100 16384-token row keeps `large_q8i_p1024` (#6442).

On the skewed 8192-token row, this change is 2.6 % faster than the previously shipped SM100 route of this row: 845.953 us against 868.6725 us, or 22.7 us less. On the uniform row it is 890.657 us against 891.553 us: 0.896 us (0.1005 %) less, inside the measurement noise, so no gain is claimed for the uniform row. The margin over FlashInfer trtllm-gen (1.43x uniform, 1.95x skew) comes from the route and is inherited from the previously shipped route. This change keeps it.
