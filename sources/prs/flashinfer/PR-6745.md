## Summary

This PR cleans up the Cake-generated BGMV LoRA-MoE shrink/expand kernels in `csrc/cake_bgmv_moe/`.

- **Kernel bodies at base:** 12 generated bodies and 2 binding headers. Four bodies are specialized (`cake_bgmv_moe_{bf16,f16}_h{2688,3072}.cu`) and eight are generic (`cake_bgmv_moe_generic_{bf16,f16}_r{8,16,32,64}.cu`).
- **Loader:** `flashinfer/jit/cake_bgmv_moe.py` generates one binding translation unit per module. That gives 48 modules: 12 bodies x 4 targets (sm90a, sm100a, sm103a, sm107a). Since this branch was first opened, `main` added three sm107a-only records with their own unfolded bodies (bf16 h2688, bf16 h3072 and the generic bf16 rank-32 merge-in-expand form); this PR leaves those three bodies untouched and keeps routing them as `main` does.
- **Host path:** `prepare_bgmv_moe(backend="cake")` and `BGMVMoECakePlan` in `flashinfer/fused_moe/bgmv_moe.py`.
