## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
FlashInfer's `deepgemm_dense_mqa` family contains the DeepSeek-V3.2 lightning-indexer logits kernels, dense `fp8_mqa_logits` and paged `fp8_paged_mqa_logits`, with FP32 accumulation and FP32 logits. Its Cake catalog has 73 generated programs, 38 dense routes and 34 paged routes. This PR re-exports the whole family at its round-4 text set (`dense_mqa.v10` → `dense_mqa.v11`), regenerating the dense and paged FP8 kernels and adding folded admission tables and an optional per-forward schedule-meta input for paged routes. It also withdraws some paged calls to FlashInfer's stock kernel. The baseline is the previous Cake export on FlashInfer `main` (a04985696294). Across paired rows the geometric-mean speedup is 1.11 on B200 (293 rows, 0.97 to 27.68) and 1.10 on GB300 (286 rows, 0.95 to 26.56), and 21 B200 rows and 19 GB300 rows are below 1.00. The largest gains are dense 64-head shapes with a long prefix: for example, Q=64 K=4164 prefix=4100 drops from 11.5 to 6.9 µs on B200 and from 10.7 to 6.4 µs on GB300.
