## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
The Cake MSA (block-sparse attention) kernels for Blackwell (`cake_blackwell_msa` backend in `flashinfer.msa_ops`) have 37 route stages per architecture: prefill union-tile (12 variants: bf16, fp16 and bf16-Q + fp8-e4m3-KV, flat and paged; folded-GQA bf16 for GQA-8 and GQA-16; paged GQA-16 causal), prefill long selected-block (3), persistent K-major prefill (3, new), decode (6: bf16, fp16 and bf16-Q + fp8-e4m3-KV, flat and paged), FP8-KV single-query decode (2), uniform-FP8 paged decode (1) and exact Top-K block selection (3). This PR re-renders all program bodies and adds the persistent K-major route (flat GQA-16, paged GQA-16, paged GQA-8) with an exact BF16 combine. Against the Cake MSA kernels on FlashInfer main, the geometric-mean speedup is 3.39× on GB300 (106 shapes) and 3.35× on B200 (105 shapes).

### Speedup

#### GB300 (106 shapes)

NVIDIA GB300 (Blackwell Ultra, sm_103a), CUDA 13.3. Microseconds are medians of 11 samples with a cold L2, two passes averaged. "previous" = the Cake MSA kernels on FlashInfer main; "this PR" = the kernels in this PR.

| shape | previous µs | this PR µs | speedup |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bf16 decode paged b128_q8_kvr752-1248 gqa8` | 261.7 | 171
