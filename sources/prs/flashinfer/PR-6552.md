## Summary

This is round 11 of the Cake `kimi_k3_fp8_projection` generated-program export: the FP8 block-scaled projection GEMMs of the Kimi-K3 KDA / MLA families, on SM100 (B200) and SM103 (GB300).

The contract has not changed:
- 352 rows per GPU.
- 198 perf rows: 22 (N, K) families × M in {1, 8, 64, 256, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096, 16384}.
- 154 correctness rows: M 3 / 129 / 513 / 1000 / 1023 / 1025 / 4097, with strided views.

The round builds on the round-10 package on FlashInfer main `ea5f7409` (#6433). That package is the "record" for every comparison below.

What this PR adds:
- Three GEMM schedule forms.
- A fix to the TMA-store epilogue of the GEMM producer. The epilogue could rewrite a staging slot before the bulk store that last read it had completed. This was the root cause of the wrong output that round 10 found for the 192-wide tile with the two-stage / four-slot TMA store. Details are under Exactness.
- 19 dispatch-table adoption entries: 9 on SM103 and 10 on SM100. That is 25 cell adoptions per GPU (24 distinct cells on SM103, 22 on SM100; a few cells were adopted in more than one step). The two newest entries adopt the 192-wide tile on `12,1,16384`, one on each GPU. T
