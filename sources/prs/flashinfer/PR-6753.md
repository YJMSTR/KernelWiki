## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
`cake_quant_lora_gemm` fuses a quantized GEMM (FP8 E4M3 or NVFP4) with a runtime BF16 low-rank LoRA term and BF16 output. The family ships 11 program variants: 6 FP8 and 5 NVFP4. This PR updates the two SM90 FP8 variants, `fp8_sm90` (form `acc2`) and `fp8_sm90_hint` (form `acc2_hint`), with a hoisted stage wait. The baseline is the previous Cake version of the same two programs, already shipped in FlashInfer. The gain is small. Over the 60 shapes below, new / previous is 0.9374 to 1.0017 cooled (median 0.9968; 59 of 60 shapes below 1.00) and 0.9794 to 1.0042 back to back (median 0.9958; 50 of 60 below 1.00).

NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3 (SXM, 700 W), sm_90a, CUDA 13 (kernels compiled with NVRTC 13.4, PyTorch cu130 runtime). CUPTI kernel time, cold L2, one run per shape: 6 back-to-back rounds and 3 cooled rounds, each round timing the previous program and then the new one. Previous and New are each program's median over the rounds; new / previous is the median of the per-round paired ratios, not the quotient of the two medians, with the per-round range of that ratio in parentheses.
