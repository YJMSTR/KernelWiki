## 📌 Description

This adds the Qwen 3.5 full-attention sub-layer as one experimental FlashInfer API, `flashinfer.qwen_3_5_block`,
served by CuTe DSL kernels vendored from cuDNN Frontend (the FROST kernels). One call runs the whole block from the post-layernorm
hidden states to the attention output: the QKV + gate projection, QK-RMSNorm with partial RoPE (fused into the projection's
epilogue; on small decode steps a lowered norm + RoPE pass), the paged-KV write-through into the serving stack's own cache,
GQA attention at head dim 256, the sigmoid output gate, and the out projection. Three serving forms over the stack's paged KV cache,
each its own compiled plan:

| form | entry point | step |
|---|---|---|
| dense prefill | `qwen_3_5_block_bf16_prefill` | a uniform `[batch, seq]` step with no cached prefix |
| extend | `qwen_3_5_block_bf16_extend` | a packed step of new tokens behind cached prefixes (chunked prefill, prefix-cache hit, speculative verify), bounded by a token capacity |
| decode | `qwen_3_5_block_bf16_decode` | a uniform `[batch, q_len]` step (`q_len = 1 + drafts`) on the Rubin d256 decode tile |

**bf16, SM107 (Rubin, cc 10.7) only** -- deliberately overfit to one m
