## 📌 Description

Two fixes for the Cake FMHA BF16 head-dim-256 small-M speculative decode route
(`decode_native_bf16_hd256_smallm`, #5369), found while serving
Qwen3.5-35B-A3B (TP=2, DFlash-7 speculative decoding) in SGLang on B200 with
CUDA graphs.

### 1. `fix(cake_fmha): order small-M corr_scale publish after PV to fix a decode deadlock`

The route can hang the GPU. The softmax warps published the rescale factor
and arrived on the `corr_scale` mbarrier **before** waiting for `p_empty`,
while QK(n+1) is issued without waiting on the correction warps. A correction
warp that started late could therefore see softmax complete `corr_scale`
phases n **and** n+1 before executing its parity wait for phase n. The parity
aliases, the correction warps never pass, `p_full` never completes, and the
CTA deadlocks — so every other Split-KV CTA spins forever in the fused merge
barrier.

(q_len 7, 8 q heads / 1 KV head, kv 60177, page 64, 148 Split-KV CTAs):
