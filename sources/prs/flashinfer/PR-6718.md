## 📌 Description

Fixes non-finite output from `backend="cake"` of `trtllm_batch_decode_with_kv_cache_mla` on the FP8 page-64 route
(`mla_fp8_page64_pdl`: FP8 E4M3, 128 heads, page 64, dense, no sink, SM100 and SM103). It happens whenever a work
item has a KV split with no tile.

**When a split is empty.** `plan_num_split` sizes the split count from the longest row (and returns at least 2). The
producer cuts each row's `tiles = ceil(len / 128)` into runs of `ceil(tiles / num_split)`, so a split starts past the
row's last tile whenever:

- a short row shares the batch with a long one, e.g. kv lens (600, 2) give 3 splits and the 2-key row has 1 tile; or
- the split count does not divide the tiles, e.g. 8 rows of 4096 keys: 9 splits of 4 tiles over 32 tiles leave split 8
  of every row empty. A single row of at most 128 keys always gets 2 splits, one of them empty.
