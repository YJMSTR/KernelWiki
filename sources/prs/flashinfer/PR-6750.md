## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
The FP4 K-grouped GEMM export (`fp4_k_grouped_gemm`) ships 21 reachable templates (9 schedule tiers × bf16 / fp32 / fp32-accumulate outputs, minus the 6 unreachable combinations). This PR delivers the `lpt` tile-order variant: the `n256_lpt` and `swapped_lpt` tiers (four templates: `n256_lpt` in bf16 / fp32 / fp32-accumulate and `swapped_lpt` in fp32), which use a host-built longest-processing-time tile table. It applies the variant to 7 shape keys and leaves every other shape on its current template. Against the previously merged version (#6428), the changed shapes run 1.034–1.120× faster on NVIDIA B200 (sm_100a) and 1.027–1.074× faster on NVIDIA GB300 (sm_103a).

NVIDIA B200 (sm_100a, CUDA 13.3) and NVIDIA GB300 (sm_103a, CUDA 13.3). Each latency is the median of 12 readings per shape. Rows at 1.000× are shapes that stay on their current template.
