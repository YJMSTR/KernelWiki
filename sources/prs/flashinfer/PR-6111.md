PR #6111 is the uniform-gated L2 stream-hint change for the `cake_fmha` on-device load-balanced ("balanced") paged-KV decode kernels. The family has 7 variant bands with 14 compiled members: BF16 and FP16 KV at head_dim 128 (each with a q_len-1 member and two packed multi-token (MTP) members, n32 and n64), E4M3 KV cache at head_dim 128 in three query/output dtypes (E4M3, BF16 or FP16 query and output), BF16 KV at head_dim 64 (8 or 16 query heads per KV head) and BF16 KV at head_dim 256 (page size 16, 32, 64). This PR adds uniform-gated per-work-item L2 stream hints to two of those sources: the q_len-1 head_dim-128 kernel (compiled for BF16 and for FP16) and the head_dim-64 BF16 kernel (compiled for 8 and for 16 query heads per KV head). When every item has the same length and every tile is split the same way, whole long items prefetch their V pages into L2 (head_dim 64 only), and split tiles load K/V under an evict_first L2 policy chosen per block through a runtime cache-policy register; heavy uniform plans (head_dim 128, 64 or more 256-token pairs per CTA) and mixed-length plans run the previous path.
