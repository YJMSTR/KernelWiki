## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
FlashInfer's `mm_bf16_fp4` multiplies BF16 activations by FP4 (E2M1) weights that carry E4M3 block scales, with FP32 accumulation. Cake ships it as two backend variants that differ in operand layout. `blackwell-native` uses cuDNN-ABI layouts and produces BF16 or FP16 output. `blackwell-tiled` uses CuTe-ABI prepared-tile layouts and produces BF16 output. This PR regenerates 3 of the 97 Cake programs of this kernel. They are the PDL programs of the large-M tcgen05 M128 pair kernel: the `blackwell-native` arm with and without alpha, and the `blackwell-tiled` arm with alpha. They replace the previous Cake generation of the same programs on `main` (#7282). That previous version is the baseline. Speedup is the previous time divided by this PR's time. Both times come from the same GPU in one job. These programs serve the 768/2112/2048 shape. There, speedup is 1.015x for `blackwell-native` and 1.029x for `blackwell-tiled` on GB300, and 1.018x and 1.023x on B200. The other 9 rows use programs this PR does not change. They measure 0.999x-1.001x on GB300 and 0.999x-1.003x on B200.
