## Summary

**Authors:** @djmmoss and @jeremylin0303

Adds SM103/B300 K96 `tcgen05` support for packed FP4 GEMMs with:

- E4M3 block-16 scale factors (NVFP4)
- E8M0 block-32 scale factors (MXFP4)
- 1-CTA and 2-CTA ABt/K-major MMA paths
- Focused K96 group-MMA coverage
- Baseline and optimized B300 GEMM samples with standalone and PyTorch extension paths

The optimized B300 sample and its pipeline improvements were authored by
@jeremylin0303.

## K96 support

This change updates:

- Shared-memory descriptors for the SM103 K96 operand layout
- Scale-factor addressing for both E4M3 and E8M0 scales
- K96 overwrite and accumulate wrappers
- The block-scaled reference GEMM used for correctness checking
- CUDA/compiler guards for the SM103 instruction forms

K96 support is currently limited to packed FP4, K-major ABt operands on SM103.
