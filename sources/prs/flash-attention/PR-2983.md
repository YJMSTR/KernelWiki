## Summary

When a KV cache contains NaNs beyond `seqused_k`, SM100 forward can return NaNs even though those positions are excluded from attention. Score masking sets their probabilities to zero, but a full-tile V load still brings the unused values into PV, where `0 * NaN` contaminates the output. Packed sequences also need their V loads bounded at each sequence boundary.

Bound each nonpaged V tile by its logical sequence length and let TMA zero-fill the unused rows before PV. The five-dimensional descriptor preserves the real Dv extent, so padded feature columns are also zero-filled. Two address coordinates preserve large aligned head/batch offsets without moving the allocation base; address arithmetic uses Int64 before conversion to bounded coordinates. Existing K loads and pipeline synchronization are preserved.
