[CuTe, SM100] S/P ping-pong to overlap QK with softmax in decode

At q_stage=1 (decode) the MMA warp had to wait for softmax to drain S and write P, and for
correction to rescale O, before issuing the next QK. S now ping-pongs between the two TMEM S/P
slots (the second was allocated but unused at q_stage=1): QK(i+1) is issued before PV(i), K/V
load in K0, K1, V0, K2, V1, ... order to match, and O_full is committed after every PV because
S(i+1) no longer implies O(i). s_p_o_stage is 2 with the ping-pong and q_stage otherwise;
FA_DISABLE_S_PING_PONG=1 turns it off. Gated to q_stage=1, hdim 64/128, 128x128 tiles, no
score_mod/mask_mod/block sparsity/sink, TMA or page-128 KV, and splits with enough KV blocks.

Based on upstream PR #2817 by @hassan-abdallah
(https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention/pull/2817), with its comments trimmed, the
duplicated slot-select blocks folded into helpers (PTX identical), and a zero local-window
bound treated as zero in the ping-pong KV-length estimate.

Co-authored-by: hassan-abdallah <hasabdallah@gmail.com>
