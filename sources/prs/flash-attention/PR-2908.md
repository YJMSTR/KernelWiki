## Summary

The softmax backward forms `dS = P * (dP - dpsum)` with `dpsum = rowsum(dO * O)`. The backward only has the bf16 output, and whenever a row's attention is peaked (one key carries most of the weight, the norm in causal DSA) `dP ~ dpsum` on the dominant key: the small difference of two large numbers is dominated by the bf16 rounding of `O`, which is coherent along the row and so does not average out in the dot product with `dO`. That error lands on the dominant key's `dS` and propagates coherently into dq and dk.

A stage-by-stage fp64 error decomposition of the sparse-MLA training path (`AI/SPARSE_MLA_DPSUM_PRECISION.md`) shows every kernel is fp32-accurate for its own inputs; the gap to fp64 is bf16 rounding, and this one term is avoidable and dominant for peaked rows: at ~80% self-attention weight dq/dk rel-L2 vs fp64 is 0.89% with ~1% of rows at ~100% error, identically in load-P and recompute-P mode and
