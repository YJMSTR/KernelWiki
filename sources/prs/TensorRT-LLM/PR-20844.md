## Description

This PR extends the Muse Spark Open modeling_v2 TEP target to mid-high concurrency on Vera Rubin (sm107, R200) at TEP4. It ports the GB300 mid-high-concurrency paths (#20770) to sm107 and adds the sm107 paths for steps of two to four requests. The changes are 9 commits:
- 7 inside the Staircase target;
- 1 small modeling_v2 router addition;
- 1 opt-in change to a shared attention backend.

**The modeling_v2 target at TEP4 on sm107.** These paths are always on inside the target and selected by GPU architecture and tensor-parallel size. (sm103, TP4), (sm103, TP8) and (sm107, TP8) serve the same paths as before. Each path owns a row band, so exactly one implementation arms per op and row count.
- Steps of two to four requests (9..32 rows):
  - wide sliding-window and full attention;
  - split-K dense projections with sm107 tables;
  - a sharded router GEMM;
  - a TP4 post-attention transport;
  - wide persistent experts;
  - half-window expert placement, behind a capacity gate at small `max_batch_size`;
  - a drafter FC inbox.
- Multi-request decode steps (five to 128 requests), ported from sm103:
  - the page-split multi-request FMHA; its CTAs per KV-head pair follow
