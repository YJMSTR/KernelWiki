## Dev Engineer Review

The change replaces Python’s fixed-tile estimate with a C++ capacity calculation that covers the enumerated KeepsMmaAb and SwapsMmaAb generation shapes. It exposes the calculation as `trtllm::spec_dec_tree_custom_mask_capacity` and passes the allocated capacity through the attention and XQA parameter structures to the FMHA runner.

`runPrepareCustomMask` now checks that capacity is positive and sufficient before dispatch. The Python path also checks that the mask is contiguous and non-empty. These checks address the reported risk of SwapsMmaAb writing beyond the allocated mask. The capacity enumeration and parameter plumbing are critical correctness paths; verify that they remain consistent with all selectable kernel shapes and tile sizes.
